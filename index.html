Hi, I’m Gianluca! I recently finished my MSc at KIT, where I worked at the Intuitive Robots Lab on fine-tuning and evaluating robotic foundation models.
I explored how past observations can be integrated into VLAs without forgetting pretrained knowledge, using lightweight finetuning or test-time steering.

I’m interested in multimodal learning, specifically temporal context modeling, learning from limited data, and cross-modal representation alignment, with application in robotics and healthcare.
